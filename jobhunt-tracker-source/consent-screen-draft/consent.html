JobHunt Tracker

Before you save your first job, here's exactly what this extension does with your data.

What changed: JobHunt Tracker now offers optional cloud sync across your devices. If you don't set it up in Settings, everything below stays exactly as before.
  • You choose what to save. The extension only reads pages where you click Scan or Save — it never crawls or opens pages on its own.
  • Your data stays in your browser. Saved jobs (title, company, location, URL, notes) and your site list live in Chrome's local storage on this device. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for ads.
  • Cloud sync is opt-in only. Your jobs leave this browser only if you set up sync yourself in Settings — it goes to a server you control, protected by your own sync key.
  • Never collected: passwords, browsing history, emails, or anything from pages you didn't ask it to read.
Full data list
  • Job postings you save: title, company, location, URL, status, notes, and any contact fields you fill in.
  • Your custom website list (set up in Settings).
  • If you enable cloud sync: the same saved-job data plus your sync credentials, sent to the backend URL you configure.
  • Chrome permissions used: storage (save your jobs), activeTab/scripting (read the job page you clicked on), alarms + notifications (follow-up reminders), and optional host access for sites you add.
